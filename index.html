Syllabus Breakdown

Course Topics & Curriculum

Systematically designed to take you from foundational cardiac electrophysiology to high-yield clinical abnormality detection.

01 Module 1

ECG Fundamentals

ECG kya hai, kaise banta hai aur iske basic principles kya hain. Understanding paper speed, voltage calibration & machine setup.

02 Module 2

Cardiac Electrical Conduction System

SA node se Purkinje fibers tak electrical impulse ka safar. Depolarization, repolarization aur myocardial vectors ki clear samajh.

03 Module 3

ECG Waves and Intervals

P wave, QRS complex, T wave, U wave aur PR interval, QRS duration, QT interval ki complete practical samajh aur normal limits.

04 Module 4

Lead System & Electrode Placement

12 leads ka concept: Standard limb leads (I, II, III), augmented leads (aVR, aVL, aVF) aur chest leads (V1-V6) ka sahi placement.

05 Module 5

Heart Rate Calculation

Heart rate nikalne ke accurate aur easy methods: 300 method, 1500 method, aur irregular rhythm mein 6-second strip technique.

06 Module 6

Rhythm Analysis

Normal sinus rhythm identify karna aur alag-alag arrhythmias (tachycardia, bradycardia, sinus pauses, ectopics) ko pehchanna.

07 Module 7

Cardiac Axis

Electrical axis kya hai, Lead I aur Lead aVF/II ke zariye kaise calculate karein aur LAD / RAD ki clinical significance kya hai.

08 Module 8

Step-by-Step ECG Interpretation

Systematic approach se ECG ko sahi tareeke se padhna: Rate, Rhythm, Axis, Hypertrophy, Ischemia/Infarction checklist.

09 Critical

Common ECG Abnormalities

Important ECG abnormalities ko samajhna aur identify karna: Ischemia, ST elevation/depression, T inversion, bundle branch blocks & infarcts.
